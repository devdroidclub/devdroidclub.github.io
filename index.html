~/devdroid/audit

audit

Something about the product feels off — drop-off in a flow, unclear SEO performance, data that doesn't add up — but it's not clear exactly where or why.

What we do

  • /Review UX: walk the real flows a user takes, flag friction points and dead ends
  • /Review SEO: technical setup, indexing, on-page basics, structured data
  • /Review data quality: check what's actually being tracked against what you think is being tracked
  • /Benchmark against what “working well” actually means for your specific product, not a generic checklist
  • /Rank findings by impact, not by how easy they are to explain

You get

  • /A written report of what we found
  • /A prioritized list of fixes, ranked by impact
  • /Nothing implemented without you deciding to — the audit is separate from the fix
