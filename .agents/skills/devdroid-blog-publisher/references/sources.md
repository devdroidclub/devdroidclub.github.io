# Source registry

Use this as a starting registry, not a closed list. Re-check current URLs and availability during each research run.

## Discovery sources

Discovery sources surface candidates but usually require confirmation from a primary source.

- Habr feed: https://habr.com/ru/feed/
- KOD (Код Дурова): https://kod.ru/
- Reddit: https://www.reddit.com/
- Awwwards: https://www.awwwards.com/
- Tagline Awards: https://tagline.ru/awards/winners/
- Rating Runeta awards: https://ratingruneta.ru/awards/about/

For Reddit, use focused communities and high-signal discussions. Treat comments as opinion or firsthand testimony, never as verified fact without corroboration. Do not reproduce long comment threads.

For Awwwards and other awards, verify the project, studio, category, year, and award on the official winner/project page. Analyze the work; do not imply that an award proves business performance.

## Preferred primary sources

### AI

- OpenAI official documentation, research, release notes, and product announcements: https://openai.com/
- Anthropic official research, documentation, news, and model notes: https://www.anthropic.com/
- Yandex AI Studio and Yandex Research: https://aistudio.yandex.ru/ and https://research.yandex.com/blog
- GigaChat developer documentation and changelog: https://developers.sber.ru/docs/ru/gigachat/changelog
- Official model cards, repositories, papers, system cards, and API documentation.

When researching OpenAI products, use the installed OpenAI documentation skill and official OpenAI sources. For technical claims about any vendor, prefer its official documentation or research paper.

### Development and platforms

- Official framework and browser documentation.
- Maintainer release notes and repositories.
- Standards bodies and security advisories.
- Engineering blogs that describe an implementation with evidence.

### Sports technology

- Official league, federation, club, venue, and technology-provider announcements.
- Product documentation, case studies with named participants, and research papers.
- Public procurement or implementation documentation when relevant and lawful to use.

Use extra care with biometric, health, athlete, and children data. Do not infer legal compliance or safety from a marketing claim.

## Source hierarchy

1. Official documentation, repository, standard, paper, award page, or named project page.
2. Named organization announcement with concrete details.
3. Reputable technical or industry publication linking to evidence.
4. Discovery publication such as Habr or KOD.
5. Community discussion such as Reddit.

Lower levels can discover a story or add perspective but should not override higher-level evidence.
